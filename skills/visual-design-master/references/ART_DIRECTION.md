# Art Direction

## Purpose

Choose a visual world before choosing UI components.

## Design thesis

Write one sentence in this form:

> **[visual tradition / mood] + [material / surface] + [contrast strategy] + [motion character]**

Examples:
- Japanese editorial × warm paper × black/red restraint × sharp but calm motion.
- Swiss modernist × white field × black/blue hierarchy × precise geometric motion.
- Playful toy packaging × saturated flat color × chunky type × springy motion.

Do not use vague directions such as “modern”, “cool”, “premium”, or “stylish” alone.

## Pick the source of visual richness

Choose at most two:
- typography;
- photography/illustration;
- geometry;
- product/UI;
- texture/material;
- data;
- character.

If everything is visually rich, nothing is dominant.

## Restraint rule

Decide what is intentionally *not* used:
- no gradients;
- no glow;
- no glassmorphism;
- no rounded cards;
- no heavy shadows;
- no texture;
- no photography;
- no decorative icons.

The absence of a device can be part of the style.

## Reference rule

For a recurring series, save 3–5 approved outputs and compare new work against:
- framing;
- density;
- typography;
- palette;
- motion rhythm;
- ending.

Do not reinvent the visual language every episode.
