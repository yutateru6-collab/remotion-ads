# Universal Visual Quality Gate

A render can be technically correct and still look weak.

## Evidence

Before delivery, inspect:
- opening;
- first major payoff;
- densest frame;
- transition/camera destination;
- ending;
- a contact sheet covering the whole piece;
- full video at normal speed.

## Scorecard

Rate 0–10:
1. Art direction coherence
2. Focal hierarchy
3. Composition / negative space
4. Typography
5. Color discipline
6. Motion purpose
7. Rhythm / holds
8. Detail polish
9. Series consistency / specificity
10. Ending / payoff

### Gate

Revise if:
- any category < 8;
- average < 8.5;
- a frame has no clear primary;
- the contact sheet looks like unrelated templates;
- text is unreadable at target size;
- any beat exists only to fill time.

## Critic questions

- What looks generic or AI-default?
- What object competes with the intended focal point?
- Where is the layout too dense or too empty?
- Which animation is decorative rather than explanatory?
- Which frame could lose 20% of its elements and improve?
- Does the piece have a recognizable visual signature?

## Technical checks

Also verify dimensions, fps, duration, audio, safe areas and file integrity. Technical QA does not replace visual QA.
